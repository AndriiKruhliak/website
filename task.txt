Create a modern multilingual website as a digital museum-style environmental exhibition dedicated to the ancient oak trees of the Chubynske landscape reserve in Ukraine.

Project title:
“Ancient Oaks of Chubynske: Living Biocultural Heritage of the Middle Dnipro Forest-Steppe”

The website should feel like a curated digital exhibition rather than a typical ecological information portal or standard photo gallery.

MAIN CONCEPT:
The project presents ancient oak trees as living elements of biocultural heritage and surviving fragments of historical forest-steppe landscapes near Kyiv. The experience should communicate silence, depth of time, natural memory, and the fragile persistence of historical landscapes under anthropogenic pressure.

The emotional atmosphere is calm, contemplative, elegant, and immersive.

The website must combine:
- museum exhibition aesthetics
- scientific environmental storytelling
- minimalist editorial design
- immersive visual presentation

The primary focus is photography.

Do NOT overload the website with text, scientific data, statistics, maps, buttons, or complex interface elements.

The website should feel premium, atmospheric, and visually clean.

LANGUAGES:
Implement full multilingual support with language switcher:
- English (main/default language)
- Ukrainian
- Czech

The language switcher should be minimalist and positioned in the top navigation.

VISUAL STYLE:
- editorial / museum minimalism
- large cinematic photography
- lots of white space
- calm natural aesthetics
- soft transitions and slow scrolling
- elegant typography
- no aggressive animations
- no bright colors
- no corporate/startup feeling
- no eco-activist visual clichés

COLOR PALETTE:
Use warm natural tones:
- warm off-white backgrounds (#F5F2EC or similar)
- deep graphite text instead of pure black
- muted forest green accents
- warm wood-brown details
- subtle earthy tones

The website should look beautiful with photography from different seasons:
spring, summer, autumn, and winter.

TYPOGRAPHY:
Headings:
- elegant editorial serif style
- similar to Cormorant Garamond, Canela, or EB Garamond

Body text:
- clean modern sans-serif
- similar to Inter, Manrope, or Suisse Intl

Typography should feel refined, academic, and contemporary.

LAYOUT:
The site should work perfectly on both desktop and mobile devices.

The mobile version is extremely important.

Use:
- full-width photography
- vertical scroll storytelling
- cinematic spacing
- minimalist navigation
- smooth transitions between sections

Avoid:
- crowded grids
- tiny thumbnails
- heavy menus
- card overload
- dashboard aesthetics

SITE STRUCTURE:

1. HERO SECTION (Landing Page)
- full-screen photograph of the most powerful ancient oak
- project title
- short subtitle (1–2 lines maximum)
- subtle scroll indicator
- minimalist top navigation

The hero section must create emotional immersion immediately.

Do NOT use sepia or vintage effects.
Use natural colors and authentic lighting.

2. SHORT INTRODUCTION SECTION
Very concise text only.

Maximum:
2–3 short paragraphs.

Topics:
- ancient oak trees
- living biocultural heritage
- preserved fragments of historical forest-steppe landscapes
- environmental and cultural significance

Tone:
scientific but poetic and restrained.

3. MAIN EXHIBITION / GALLERY
This is the core of the website.

Create a slow immersive gallery experience.

Presentation style:
- large photographs
- one image dominates the screen at a time
- breathing space between images
- vertical scrolling narrative
- minimalist transitions

The gallery should feel like walking through a digital exhibition.

Do NOT create:
- dense photo grids
- Pinterest-like layouts
- commercial portfolio aesthetics

Photographs:
- approximately 10–15 main hero images
- optional additional archive section

Photo captions:
Keep extremely minimal.

Optional:
very short atmospheric phrases between sections.

Examples:
“Fragments of a living landscape”
“Old-growth forest memory”
“Biocultural continuity”



AUTHORSHIP:
Every photograph must contain a very subtle photographer credit directly on the image in a lower corner.

Format examples:
© Oksana Shcherbak, 2026
© O.S., 2026

The credit should be elegant, discreet, and unobtrusive.

4. LANDSCAPE CONTEXT SECTION
A small atmospheric section with:
- several supporting landscape images
- short text about the Middle Dnipro forest-steppe
- mention anthropogenic pressure near urbanized areas

Keep the text concise.

5. PAVLO CHUBYNSKYI SECTION
Minimal cultural context section.

Do NOT create a full biography.

Focus on:
- symbolic connection between landscape, memory, and identity
- cultural significance of the territory
- historical and intellectual context

Include:
- one portrait or historical image if available
- very short text (4–6 sentences maximum)

6. ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS SECTION
Minimalist section with:
- gratitude to Czech partners/supporters
- mention of initial project support
- optional institutional logos

Keep visually subtle and elegant.

7. FOOTER
Include:
- institution name
- project credits
- FoPD 2026 reference
- optional social links

UX PRINCIPLES:
The entire website should feel like:
- a contemplative digital environmental exhibition
- a slow visual narrative
- an atmospheric museum experience

The user should feel:
- immersion
- silence
- continuity of time
- fragility and value of preserved landscapes

IMPORTANT:
Less text, more atmosphere.
Less interface, more visual storytelling.
Less information density, more emotional and aesthetic impact.

The website should communicate scientific and cultural value through visual experience rather than through large amounts of explanatory text.

The final result should feel contemporary, elegant, internationally presentable, and suitable for environmental humanities, museum presentation, or international cultural-scientific initiatives.


Avoid looking like:
- a commercial photography portfolio
- a startup landing page
- a scientific database
- a tourism website
- an NGO environmental campaign page

The website should instead resemble:
- a contemporary digital museum exhibition
- a visual environmental humanities project
- a curated nature art-science experience
